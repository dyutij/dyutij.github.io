My teaching connects communication theory with real-world contexts through active learning, reflection, and practical application.

## Teaching areas

- Organizational Communication
- Small Group Communication
- Public Speaking
- Communication Technology
- Research Methods

## Teaching philosophy

I aim to create inclusive learning environments that encourage students to examine assumptions, communicate thoughtfully, and develop analytical skills.
